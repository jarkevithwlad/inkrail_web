Inkrail typewriter recordings

The six presets use separate recordings. Each set of three letter keys is
cut from three different strikes on the same machine. The recordists
released these sounds under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Classic — Hermes Precisa 305
Joseph SARDIN, BigSoundBank. “Typewriter #2” provides the three keys and
the carriage return. “Typewriter, space” and “Typewriter, Bell #1” provide
the space bar and margin bell.
https://bigsoundbank.com/typewriter-2-s2835.html
https://bigsoundbank.com/typewriter-space-s2843.html
https://bigsoundbank.com/typewriter-bell-s2844.html

Soft — Hermes 3000
supersupernator, “supersuper_Typewriter.wav”. Three keys and the bell,
with high frequencies gently rolled off for quieter writing.
https://freesound.org/people/supersupernator/sounds/519622/

Royal — Royal Quiet De Luxe
videog, “Typing on a typewriter”. Three keys, the margin bell and the
following carriage return.
https://freesound.org/people/videog/sounds/240839/

Vintage — 1930s manual typewriter
monotraum, “old typewriter.wav”. Three keys and the hand-operated return.
https://freesound.org/people/monotraum/sounds/434027/

Portable — Hermes Baby
evsecrets, “Typing on a typewriter with ding at end of each line.wav”.
Three keys, the bell and the carriage slide and stop.
https://freesound.org/people/evsecrets/sounds/334458/

Electric — IBM Selectric II
secretmojo, “Typewriter, IBM Selectric II”. Three powered print cycles.
The recordist describes this machine's bell as broken. Inkrail supplies
its own margin bell and powered return.
https://freesound.org/people/secretmojo/sounds/224012/

Inkrail trimmed the recordings, mixed them to mono, filtered background
noise, adjusted levels and faded the cuts. The other 15 sounds are original
designs for the individual machines.
